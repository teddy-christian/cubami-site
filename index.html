Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the mobile game Cubby (package name com.cubby.game), published by Cubby Games. For questions, write to cubbygames05@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

It describes how the current version of Cubby (version 2.4) handles information. Cubby is a single-player block-puzzle game that you can play entirely offline.

3. We do not collect personal data

The current version of Cubby does not collect, share, or sell personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting, and no tracking. We do not send information from the game to our servers or to third parties, because this version does not use any servers for gameplay.

Your best score, language choice, and sound, music, and vibration settings are stored only on your device. They never leave the device. If you uninstall the game, that information is deleted with it.

4. Ads and in-app purchases

The current version of Cubby shows no ads and offers no in-app purchases. You can play the full game without paying and without watching advertisements.

Future versions. A later update may show ads (for example a banner or an occasional full-screen ad after a game, and an optional rewarded ad to revive). If that happens, we will update this privacy policy and the Google Play listing before those features go live, and we will declare ads in Play Console. We will not start collecting personal data without updating this policy.

5. Permissions

The Android app may request these permissions:

  • Internet and network state — not required to play. The game works offline. These permissions are reserved for a possible future update (for example ads) and are not used to collect your data in this version.
  • Vibration — only if you leave vibration on in Settings. You can turn it off at any time.

6. Children

Cubby does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. The current version is a casual puzzle with no ads, no chat, and no data collection.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date below. The updated policy will apply to later versions of the game.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, email cubbygames05@gmail.com.
